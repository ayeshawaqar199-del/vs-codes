SmartHire: AI-Assisted Job Matching & CV Recommendation System

Authored by Ayesha Waqar. Solving traditional keyword-match limitations with Context-Aware spaCy & Sentence Transformers NLP models.

The Fit Problem in Hiring

Traditional portals rely on rigid keyword queries. Qualified applicants are missed due to variations like 'Software Engineer' vs 'Backend Developer' or missing explicit acronyms.

  • High manual screening effort for HR recruiters
  • Keyword stuffing gaming the traditional ATS
  • Unstructured CV parsing failures

System Architecture

SmartHire uses multi-stage processing: SpaCy NLP pipeline extracts Entities & Skills -> Vector Embedding via Sentence-BERT -> Weighted Score Matrix calculation.

[User CV / Job Specs] ➔ [spaCy Tokenizer & NER] ➔ [Skill Vector Embedding] ➔ [Cosine Distance Engine] ➔ [Ranked Candidate Output]
